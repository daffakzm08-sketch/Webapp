Cookie cf_clearance

y2mate.com dilindungi Cloudflare. Buka y2mate.com di Chrome, lolos verifikasi, lalu salin nilai cookie cf_clearance (DevTools → Application → Cookies).

Cookie cepat kedaluwarsa. Isi ulang bila muncul pesan ditolak.
